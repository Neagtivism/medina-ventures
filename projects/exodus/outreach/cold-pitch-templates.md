# Cold Pitch Templates

Fill in `[bracketed]` fields per business. Keep the first touch short — the goal is the demo/appointment, not a close.

## In-person (walk-in)

> Hi, I'm [name] — quick one, do you handle your Google reviews yourself, or does someone else? [pause for answer]
>
> Cool — I put a little tap card at the counter of businesses like yours that makes it a 3-second tap for a happy customer to leave a review, instead of them having to search you up later and forgetting. Most places see review volume jump pretty fast once it's frictionless.
>
> I've got one on me — mind if I show you? Takes 30 seconds. [demo the tap]
>
> If you want one, first month's free so you can see the review count move before committing to anything.

## Email

**Subject options:**
- Quick question about [Business Name]'s Google reviews
- 3-second review tap for [Business Name]
- [Referral name] suggested I reach out

**Body:**
> Hi [Owner name],
>
> I work with local businesses in [area] on getting more Google reviews without adding work for staff — a small NFC card at the counter/register that lets a happy customer leave a review with one tap, no typing your name into their phone.
>
> Happy to bring one by for a 5-minute demo, no pitch, no cost to look. First month's free if you want to try it after that.
>
> Worth a quick chat this week?
>
> [Name] · [Phone]

## SMS / follow-up (after a demo or no-response)

> Hey [Owner name], [Name] here from [visit context] — just following up on the review card. No rush, happy to answer questions or swing back by. Let me know!

**No-response, second follow-up (5-7 days later):**
> Hi again — totally fine if now's not the time. If you want to revisit later just text me, otherwise no more follow-ups from me. Good luck with the reviews either way 🙂

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already have good reviews." | "That's great — this just keeps the volume climbing instead of plateauing. More recent reviews also rank better with Google's algorithm." |
| "How much does this cost?" | "First month's free so you can see it working before any commitment. After that it's the retainer we discussed — [rate] — which also includes [analytics/upsell scope]." |
| "Not interested right now." | "No worries — mind if I check back in a month? No pressure either way." |
| "Does this actually work / isn't this a gimmick?" | Offer to demo live on the spot with the owner's own phone — removes the skepticism fastest. |
| "We don't have budget." | Lead with the free first month again — zero-risk trial removes the budget objection until they've seen results. |
